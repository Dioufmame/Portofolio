Case study · Orange CI

Predicting underperforming sales partners in the fibre business

Project · Performance analysis of door-to-door fibre sales partners and modelling of the drivers of underperformance

The starting point: not a single door-to-door sales partner was hitting its monthly fibre sales target, despite bonuses, training and tracking tools. I analysed their sales from January 2024 to July 2025 and modelled the drivers of underperformance. The results were used to redesign the partners' territories.

Confidentiality. No data and no partner names are published. The variables, method and model metrics are those of the real project. The simulator uses fictitious coefficients, for illustration only.

  1. Frame

    Define the question

    Binary target: performing or underperforming partner. Goal: explain underperformance and be able to anticipate it.

  2. Collect

    Build the dataset

    Extraction of fibre sales, targets and partner information from internal systems.

  3. Prepare

    Clean and enrich

    In R: missing values, duplicates, date formats, removal of identifying columns, joins to add tenure and location.

  4. Explore

    Profiles and map

    Sales trends and volatility over 18 months, performance profiles, mapping of geographic clusters in QGIS.

  5. Explain

    Four variables

    Tenure in months, location frequency (how dynamic the area is), volatility of target achievement, number of competitors in the city. All standardised.

  6. Model

    Three models compared

    Logistic regression, Random Forest and XGBoost. Stratified 70 / 30 split, class rebalancing with SMOTE, 5-fold cross-validation.

  7. Choose

    Keep the logistic model

    The only model that genuinely detects performing partners, with the best F1, and interpretable: every effect turns into a decision.

  8. Decide

    Territories and training

    Territories reassigned according to partner profiles. The weaker partners were then trained through the fibre academy, leading to better-quality sales.

What the model saysestimated effects · logistic regression
  • ▲▲
    Location frequency

    Strong positive effect: partners placed in dynamic areas succeed far more often. Territory assignment is an immediate lever.

  • ▲
    Tenure

    Experience improves performance: process mastery and knowledge of the field.

  • ▲
    Sales volatility

    An unexpected positive effect: irregular partners who can peak during campaigns.

  • ▼
    Number of competitors

    Performance drops in highly contested areas, which call for more seasoned profiles.

The decision

The right partner in the right territory

Rather than penalising weaker partners, the study placed them where they could improve.

Highly competitive areas
Experienced partners

Longer tenure, with the teams and resources to hold their ground against competitors.

Less competitive areas and inland regions
Weaker partners

A more accessible market to regain momentum, with dedicated training.

Training through the fibre academy

At the senior manager's request, the weaker partners were trained through the fibre academy in sales techniques and the best practices of top performers. I also recommended mentoring of newcomers by experienced partners, and targeting "irregular but dynamic" partners for campaigns.

What the training changed
  • Better-quality sales, made in areas actually covered by fibre.
  • Accurate customer location, making technician scheduling and installation easier.
  • Fewer pending sales orders.
  • Fewer penalties for partners' bad sales.
  • A better customer experience.

My method, from business to data

From business need to a solution that runs on its own.

As an econometrician by training, I always start with the business question before touching any code.

  1. Understand

    I turn the teams' question into measurable KPIs and hypotheses to test.

    business workshops · KPIs

  2. Make it reliable

    I collect, cross-check and validate data from every source before any analysis.

    SQL · ETL · quality

  3. Model

    Statistics, segmentation, predictive models: the method follows the problem, never the other way round.

    R · Python · ML

  4. Automate

    The result becomes a documented dashboard or scheduled pipeline, and the teams are trained to use it.

    Power BI · Shiny · CI/CD
